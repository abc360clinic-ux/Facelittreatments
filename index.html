What is exosome hair treatment?

Exosomes are cell-derived vesicles involved in cell communication. Their use in hair treatments is an evolving area, and suitability and expected outcomes can differ from person to person.

At Facelit, a clinician can explain the procedure, discuss available evidence and alternatives, and help you decide what questions to consider before proceeding.

Frequently asked questions

Will exosome hair treatment be right for me?

It depends on your individual situation and medical history. A clinician can assess your concerns and explain suitable options.

What happens during a consultation?

You can discuss your concerns, ask about the procedure, and learn about suitability, costs, care and alternatives before making a decision.

When might I see a change?

Outcomes and timelines vary. Ask your clinician what is realistic for your situation; no result or timeframe can be guaranteed.

Where can I ask about availability and cost?

Request a consultation and select Bangalore, Kalyan Nagar or Trivandrum, Kowdiar. The clinic team can discuss current availability and pricing.
