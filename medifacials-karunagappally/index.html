Frequently Asked Questions

What is a medifacial?

A medifacial is a clinic-based facial that goes beyond a regular salon facial. It uses medical-grade devices and active ingredients, chosen after your skin has been assessed by a doctor.

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. Every plan starts with a consultation where the doctor understands your skin concerns, skin type and goals, and then recommends the treatment that suits you.

Which treatments do you offer?

Medifacials, HydraFacial, chemical peels, microneedling and Pico laser, among others. Which one is right for you is decided at your consultation.

Is it suitable for Kerala’s humid, sunny climate?

Yes. Your plan takes your skin type and our local climate into account, since humidity, sun exposure and sweat all affect skin concerns like dullness, pigmentation and acne.

Is there any downtime?

It depends on the treatment. Gentle treatments like HydraFacial usually let you return to your routine the same day, while peels or laser can cause temporary redness or peeling. Your doctor will tell you what to expect.

How many sessions will I need?

This varies from person to person. The doctor advises on the number of sessions after assessing your skin. Results vary between individuals.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the treatment and the number of sessions. You’ll get clear information at your consultation, before you decide on anything.
