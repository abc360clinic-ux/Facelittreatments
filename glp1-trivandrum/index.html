GLP-1 treatment: your questions answered

Am I suitable for GLP-1 treatment?

That is decided by the doctor after a consultation and relevant tests. It is not suitable for everyone, and your doctor will tell you honestly if another approach is better for you.

Are there side effects?

Some people experience effects such as nausea or digestive discomfort. Your doctor will explain these before you start and guide you on managing them.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on your plan. You will get clear pricing at your consultation, before you decide on anything.

How soon will I see results?

This varies from person to person. Your doctor will explain what to realistically expect for your body and goals.

Will I need to stay on it forever?

Your doctor will discuss how long treatment is likely to last for you and what happens after, so you know the plan before you start.

What is GLP-1 therapy?

GLP-1 medicines are prescription medicines that act on appetite and blood sugar regulation. They can help with weight loss when prescribed to the right person and used under medical supervision.

Will the plan work with Kerala food?

Yes. Your nutrition plan is built around what you actually eat, such as rice, fish, coconut-based curries and festival meals, rather than a generic diet chart.

Who supervises my treatment?

Your plan, dosing and follow-ups are managed by a doctor and adjusted based on how your body responds.
