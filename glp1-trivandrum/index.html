Frequently Asked Questions

What is GLP therapy?

GLP medicines are prescription medicines that act on appetite and blood sugar regulation. They can help with weight loss when prescribed to the right person and used under medical supervision.

Is the programme suited to Kerala food and lifestyle?

Yes. Your nutrition plan is built around the food you actually eat, such as rice, fish, coconut-based curries and festival meals, rather than a generic diet chart.

Am I suitable for it?

That is decided by the doctor after a consultation and relevant tests. It is not suitable for everyone, and your doctor will tell you if another approach is better.

Is it doctor-supervised?

Yes. Your plan, dosing and follow-ups are managed by a doctor, and adjusted based on how your body responds.

How soon will I see results?

This varies from person to person. Your doctor will explain what to realistically expect for your body and goals.

Are there side effects?

Some people experience effects such as nausea or digestive discomfort. Your doctor will explain these before you start and guide you on managing them.

Will I need to stay on it forever?

The aim is to help you build habits and reach a result you can maintain. Your doctor will discuss the duration and a maintenance plan with you.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on your plan. You will get clear information at your consultation, before you decide on anything.
